# ReelGuard — GitHub Repository & Release Descriptions

## 1. Short Repository "About" Description (One-Line)
Real-time Android Reel & Shorts counter with a top-of-screen floating overlay, green-to-red threshold alert, animated Brain Health indicator, and Day/Week/Month analytics across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and all short-video apps.

## 2. Short GitHub Release Description (`v1.0.0`)
**ReelGuard** is a lightweight Android app that automatically counts the Reels and Shorts you watch across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and any other short-video platform.

### Key Features
* **Smart Auto-Overlay**: Displays a floating reel counter at the top of your screen only while you are actively watching Reels or Shorts, and hides automatically when you leave.
* **Green-to-Red Visual Alert & Brain Health Animation**: Shifts smoothly from Emerald Green to Warning Amber to Crimson Red as you approach your daily limit, paired with a live animated brain graphic that degrades as your count rises and dynamically updates your home screen app icon.
* **Accurate Swipe Detection**: Uses static per-video fingerprinting so pausing a video or watching long videos with subtitles never triggers false counts.
* **Day, Week & Month Dashboard**: Tracks your total reels watched today, over the last 7 days, and over the last 30 days, with a per-platform breakdown using each app's native icon.

### Installation & Setup
1. Download **`ReelGuard.apk`** (or **`app-debug.apk`**) under **Assets** below and install it on your Android phone.
2. Open **ReelGuard** and tap **1. Enable Overlay** to grant the *Display over other apps* permission.
3. Tap **2. Enable Detector** and turn on **ReelGuard Reel & Shorts Detector** in your Android Accessibility Settings.
